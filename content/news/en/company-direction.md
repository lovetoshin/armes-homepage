ARMES has clearly defined its identity as an **everyday-life service company that removes daily inconveniences** and redesigned its website to reflect this company-first approach.

ARMES is not a company that researches technology itself — it is a company that **uses data, automation, and location technology as tools to build services that touch real everyday life**. While we have previously introduced ourselves through individual services, we now aim to present in one place how the company uses these technologies and what services it is building.

## Technologies We Use

- **Data** — Collecting scattered information, organizing it, and processing it into something useful
- **Automation** — Automating repetitive tasks so people don't have to
- **Vision** — Technology that analyzes and classifies photos
- **Location** — Region and location-based services

## Services We Build

More than the technology itself, what we do is create **services where that technology actually makes a difference**.

- **Live** — SellerAI (e-commerce image automation), ARMES Tools (free online utilities), RewardTalk (local membership), TravelMoa (travel deals), CocoPing (Costco discounts)
- **In Development** — WorldLingo (online language lessons), Husamsukji (turn-based strategy simulation game), PhotoSort (automatic photo classification), RankingPangPang (ranking content)

For more details, please visit the [Projects](/#projects) section.
